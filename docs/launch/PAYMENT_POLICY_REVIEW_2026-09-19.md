# TrainWith payment and store-policy review

**Historical baseline:** the findings below describe commit `9330325`. The subsequent requested fixes and the selected US-first iOS model are documented in [COMPLIANCE_IMPLEMENTATION.md](COMPLIANCE_IMPLEMENTATION.md). Unverified store settings and signed-device acceptance remain open.

Reviewed 19 September 2026 against source commit `9330325` and current official documentation. Scope: the connected app's payment, account, creator-content and submission flows. No App Store Connect pricing, storefront availability or signed binary was verified in this review. This is a readiness assessment, not an Apple approval or a certification of every jurisdiction's laws.

## Decision

**RevenueCat is optional. TrainWith is not ready to be declared store-compliant.** The reason is the unfinished payment/distribution model and specific product gaps below, not the absence of RevenueCat.

TrainWith can be free to download; configure and verify that price in App Store Connect. App download pricing is a separate setting from creator memberships. [Apple pricing setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/)

The product sells access to recorded workout videos and programs. That is digital content. A free download or paying proceeds to creators does not create an IAP exemption. The real-time, one-to-one service exception does not describe this library. [Apple payment rules, 3.1.1 and 3.1.3(d)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#payments)

RevenueCat helps manage purchases and subscription access; it is not the payment processor or the creator payout system. Store billing can also be implemented directly. TrainWith's current backend already tracks Stripe memberships without RevenueCat. [RevenueCat overview](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/welcome/overview), [payment responsibility](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/platform-resources/developer-store-payments)

## Which Stripe approach can work?

| Distribution          | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TrainWith website     | Continue Stripe Checkout and Connect. The repository configures recurring payments with a connected creator as the destination. Real payments still need activation and acceptance tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| US iOS storefront     | A browser-based Stripe purchase flow is a viable route. Apple permits external purchase links here without the special link entitlement. Implement storefront eligibility, browser Checkout and a reliable return to the app before enabling it. This is a proposed route, not implemented behavior. [Apple US update](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=9txfddzf), [Stripe's iOS integration guide](https://docs.stripe.com/mobile/digital-goods/checkout)                                                                                                  |
| Other iOS storefronts | Do not copy the US flow worldwide. Choose IAP or a documented applicable exception/regional program. Availability and terms must be assessed for the selected markets. [Apple payment rules](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#payments)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Reader-app approach   | Previously purchased video access may qualify when media consumption is the primary function. TrainWith's eligibility has not been established. Reader linking outside the US has conditions; simply disabling the purchase button does not establish eligibility. [Apple reader-app requirements](https://developer.apple.com/support/reader-apps/)                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Google Play           | Separate assessment required. Fitness subscriptions are digital purchases under Play's policy; eligible alternatives require the applicable program. The US alternative-billing program includes enrollment/integration obligations and announced reporting/fee changes from 1 October 2026. Do not assume the iOS implementation covers Android. [Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en), [US alternative billing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16497028?hl=en) |

For a Stripe-first launch, my recommendation is **web first, then a US-first iOS release after the listed fixes**. Launch markets remain unconfirmed. No distribution settings were changed.
